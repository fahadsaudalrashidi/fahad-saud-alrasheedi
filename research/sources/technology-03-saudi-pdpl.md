## Extracted findings

The Saudi Personal Data Protection Law (PDPL) provisions regarding your requested topics are as follows:

**Personal Data Processing**
*   **Definition:** Includes any operation carried out on Personal Data by manual or automated means, such as collecting, recording, storing, using, disclosing, or destroying data [L29-L32].
*   **Consent:** Generally required for Processing, with specific conditions to be set by Regulations [L126-L128]. Processing can proceed without consent in specific cases, including legitimate interests (non-sensitive data), judicial requirements, or public interest [L137-L154, L196-L215].
*   **Principles:** Data must be processed for the purposes it was collected for [L190-L191], be appropriate and limited to the minimum amount necessary [L229-L230], and remain accurate/relevant [L278-L280].

**International Transfers**
*   Controllers may transfer or disclose Personal Data outside the Kingdom under specific conditions, such as fulfilling international agreements, serving Kingdom interests, or performing obligations to which the Data Subject is a party [L475-L485].
*   Requirements include: ensuring no prejudice to national security, an adequate level of protection (at least equivalent to the Law), and limiting the transfer to the minimum necessary data [L487-L501].

**Data-Subject Rights**
*   **Information:** Right to be informed of the legal basis and purpose of collection [L108-L109].
*   **Access:** Right to access Personal Data held by the Controller [L111-L112].
*   **Data Portability:** Right to obtain data in a readable and clear format [L115-L116].
*   **Correction:** Right to request correction, completion, or updating of data [L117-L118].
*   **Destruction:** Right to request destruction of data when no longer needed [L120-L122].

**Controllers**
*   **Definition:** Any Public Entity, natural person, or private legal person that specifies the purpose and manner of Processing [L75-L77].
*   **Responsibilities:** Must implement organizational and technical measures for protection [L371-L373], perform impact assessments [L391-L393], maintain records of processing activities [L552-L555], and notify the Competent Authority and Data Subjects of breaches [L377-L382].
*   **Processors:** Controllers must select Processors that provide necessary guarantees and monitor their compliance [L165-L168].

**AI Support Operations**
*   While the provided text does not explicitly mention "AI," the Law applies to any manual or automated Processing [L29]. Controllers are mandated to conduct impact assessments for products or services based on the nature of their activity [L391-L393], which would encompass AI-driven operations.
