## Extracted findings

MEMERAG is a multilingual, end-to-end meta-evaluation benchmark for Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) designed to address the lack of non-English benchmarks that capture cultural nuances [L14].

*   **Language Coverage:** The benchmark utilizes the MIRACL dataset and emphasizes the use of native-language questions rather than translations to ensure an authentic user experience [L14].
*   **Evaluation Dimensions:** The benchmark assesses RAG systems based on two primary dimensions: **faithfulness** and **relevance**, using expert human annotators [L14].
*   **Findings:** The study demonstrates that MEMERAG achieves high inter-annotator agreement and can reliably identify performance improvements provided by advanced prompting techniques and different LLMs [L14]. 
*   **Limitations:** The provided text does not explicitly detail specific limitations of the benchmark, though it notes that existing benchmarks often fail because they rely on English or translated data, which MEMERAG seeks to overcome [L14].
