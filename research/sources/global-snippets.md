## Synthesis and use in the brief
- The strongest fetched public evidence here is Gartner's 2025 customer survey (5,801 customers) and an ICMI online survey of 113 service/IT-support professionals. The latter reports 54% using some AI functionality, 36% planning future introduction and 10% with no plans; margin of error reported as ±9 points. The ICMI report is hosted by NICE and should be described as industry survey evidence, not wholly independent proof.
- Gartner's public abstract reports a $43.4B 2024 CRM customer-service/support software estimate and 13.9% constant-currency CAGR forecast through 2028. This is explicitly an analyst forecast, not a new estimate; full methodology is access-restricted and the figure should be cited as Gartner's forecast, never generalized to all customer-operations/ESM software.
- Public material supports interest in AI, measurable CX/efficiency outcomes, and extending service practices beyond IT; it does not establish one global ESM market size, willingness to pay, Saudi/MENA-specific demand, or that projected benefits are realized. The available sources are cross-sectional, geographically mixed, and often vendor/consultant-sponsored.
- Product implication: frame AI as augmenting human resolution and decision quality, with auditable workflow outcomes; validate regional demand with Saudi/GCC buyer interviews and procurement data rather than extrapolating global forecasts.
