Since the launch of Vision 2030, unprecedented changes throughout Saudi Arabia have reshaped what it means to live in the Kingdom. Such remarkable change and growth are driven in part by the National Transformation Program. From digitizing government services to save time and effort, to promoting an inclusive private sector which prioritizes your financial security, the Program is creating a diversified, innovative, and world-leading nation.

![Loading...](https://www.vision2030.gov.sa/_next/image?url=%2Fassets%2Fimg%2Fbg%2Fpattern-bg.png&w=3840&q=75)

![Saudi Vision 2030](https://www.vision2030.gov.sa/media/vx4h0415/artboard-3.png)

Launched in 2016, the National Transformation Program was the first Vision Realization Program established under Vision 2030. The Program works tirelessly to achieve strategic objectives that are transforming Saudi Arabia into a leading nation. This covers a range of themes, including empowering the private sector, achieving government excellence, and developing economic partnerships.

The Program is accelerating the Kingdom’s digital transformation, too: from starting an online business in just three minutes to attending court sessions from the comfort of your home, changes like these help meet your needs, wherever you are.

Today’s workplace is more accessible and inclusive than ever, thanks to a series of initiatives launched by the Program. With employment opportunities in sectors that didn’t exist six years ago, Saudi women are joining the workforce at an unprecedented pace, while employment rates for disabled people are at a record high.

The values of giving and compassion are firmly rooted in Saudi society, which is why the Program is actively developing the non-profit sector and creating platforms like [the National Volunteer Portal](https://www.vision2030.gov.sa/en/explore-more/national-volunteer-portal/ "Volunteer Portal"). Volunteerism in Saudi Arabia has seen a 30-fold increase as more and more people give back to their community.

As we reach the halfway mark of Vision 2030, the Program continues to deliver its promise of building a better country and fulfilling our dream of prosperity.
