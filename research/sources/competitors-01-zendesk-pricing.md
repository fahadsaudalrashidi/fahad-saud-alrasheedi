## Frequently asked questions, answered.

##### How is Zendesk pricing structured?

Zendesk pricing is primarily seat-based (per agent, per month) with plan tiers that include different features. Your total cost typically includes:

- Base subscription: Pricing is determined by your selected plan tier and the number of agent seats needed.

- Usage-based features: Certain features like App Builder, Action Builder and Voice  are billed based on consumption exceeding applicable plan allowances.

- Add-ons: Optional products and advanced capabilities are billed in addition to your base plan.

- Annual vs. monthly billing: Annual subscriptions often include a discount compared with paying month-to-month.


[Contact Sales](https://www.zendesk.com/contact/) to learn more.

##### What is the difference between Support and Suite Service Plans?

- Support Plans: If you are just starting out with email-based customer support, the Support plan is a great place to begin. It covers the essentials with the flexibility to add channels, expand capabilities, or upgrade to the full Zendesk Suite as your needs grow.


- Suite Plans: The Zendesk Suite is your all-in-one customer service solution. Equipped with ticketing, messaging, live chat, help center, and voice support, it is ideal for businesses providing multi-channel customer support. All Suite plans include powerful automations and AI agents available 24/7 to resolve customer issues quickly and effectively.


Learn more about [Support plans](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408893293466-About-Zendesk-Support-plan-types) and [Suite plans](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408846875034-About-the-Zendesk-Suite-plan-types) as the different Service Plan levels available or contact Sales if you’re evaluating enterprise requirements.

##### How does pricing for AI agents work?

Zendesk AI agents are included in every Suite and Support plan, with pricing based on the successful outcomes they deliver.

- **Automated Resolutions:** Are the unit of measurement used for calculating and billing your account for AI agent usage. Paying per automated resolution means that you pay only for customer requests that were successfully resolved by the AI agent, without any escalation to a human agent.


[Learn more](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/5352026794010-About-automated-resolutions-for-AI-agents#topic_t1f_bqb_g2c)

##### What are Forethought AI agents?

Forethought AI agents by Zendesk are self-improving AI agents that can understand customer requests, take action, and continuously improve outcomes over time. These agents bring specialized AI into the Zendesk AI ecosystem and can handle more complex workflows across all channels and environments.

They’re built to work seamlessly within Zendesk for those looking to elevate their service experience—and, just as importantly, they work on any platform, making it easy to bring best-in-class AI agents to an existing stack.

[Contact Sales](https://www.zendesk.com/contact/) to learn more.

##### What add-ons are available and how are they priced?

Zendesk offers add-ons that extend your Service Plan with additional functionality such as advanced capabilities, additional channels, or specialized products.

- How add-ons are priced: Add-ons are typically priced separately from your base plan and may be billed by seats, usage, or the specific add-on.

- Availability: Add-ons can vary by plan tier and by purchase path (self-serve vs. sales-assisted).


Explore options: View all available add-ons [here](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408834152730-About-Zendesk-product-add-ons).

##### How can I purchase Zendesk?

There are two main ways to buy Zendesk:

- Online purchase: Best for individuals and teams who want to get started quickly. Typically paid by credit card during checkout.

- Sales-assisted: Best for larger organizations, complex requirements, or enterprise plans. Typically involves working with our sales team and may support invoice-based billing depending on eligibility and terms.

- Payment methods: Bank account (direct debit), Credit or debit card, or PayPal. For all other types of payments, contact your account representative.

- Supported currency: Currently, Zendesk supports USD, EUR, GBP, and BRL.


Unsure? [Contact Sales](https://www.zendesk.com/contact/) to confirm the right Service Plan and purchasing method.

##### Can I purchase enterprise plans online?

Enterprise Service Plans generally require sales engagement. If you’re looking for an enterprise plan, the best next step is to contact Sales so we can help you evaluate the right package, confirm requirements, and set up purchasing (often including invoice options where applicable).

[Contact Sales](https://www.zendesk.com/contact/)

##### How long are your contracts? Can I upgrade or downgrade?

Zendesk subscriptions are month to month, annual, or multi-year. You can reach out to your Account Executive/Sales Representative for additional billing options.

- Monthly billing: More flexibility, typically a higher effective monthly rate.

- Annual billing: Usually discounted compared to paying month-to-month.

- Upgrades/downgrades: Service Plan changes can affect billing and may apply immediately or at renewal depending on your subscription terms.


[Learn more here](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408834640666-Viewing-and-managing-plan-subscriptions) or [contact us for details](https://www.zendesk.com/contact/).

##### How does the free trial work?

Zendesk offers a free trial so you can explore the product before committing to a paid plan. During the 14 day trial period:

- Default plan: You'll have access to all features on the Zendesk Customer Service Suite Professional plan for 14 days. If you want to trial a specific plan contact us. If you want to trial an Employee Service Suite plan see [here](https://www.zendesk.com/register/?source=employee_service_suite).

- Add-ons: Copilot is included by default. If you’d like to try other add-ons please contact sales.

- AI access: Trial access to AI features is available for evaluation purposes, though the capabilities included may vary.


Learn more: [Free trial details and what’s included](https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/9104802071578-Welcome-to-your-Zendesk-Suite-trial).

##### How do I estimate my total cost?

To estimate your total Zendesk cost, consider these main components:

- Number of agents (seats): Base plan cost is typically per agent/month.

- Add-ons: Include any add-ons you need.

- AI agents resolutions: If you use AI agents to automate resolutions, factor in expected resolutions exceeding your plan allowance.

- Monthly vs. annual billing: Annual billing often reduces the overall price compared with month-to-month.


Helpful tool: Use the [ROI calculator](https://www.zendesk.com/service/ai/ai-agents/#ai-roi-calculator).

##### Does Zendesk integrate with other applications and systems?

Yes. Zendesk integrates with a wide range of tools and platforms, and you can connect to other systems in a few ways:

- Prebuilt integrations: Available through the Zendesk Marketplace (apps and integrations).

- Custom integrations: Supported via APIs for building tailored workflows and data connections.

- Enterprise integrations: Larger organizations often implement deeper integrations (for example with CRM, identity providers, data platforms, or custom internal systems).


Explore the [Zendesk Marketplace](https://www.zendesk.com/marketplace/) or review our [API documentation](https://developer.zendesk.com/documentation/).

##### How do you protect customer data?

Zendesk is built with enterprise-grade security and privacy to help protect customer data. We combine robust security features with comprehensive audits of our applications, systems, and networks to help ensure customer and business data remains protected.

Visit the [Zendesk Trust Center](https://www.zendesk.com/trust-center/) for the most up-to-date information and documentation on security and privacy practices, compliance and certifications, and product and infrastructure security controls.
