# Forecast Analysis: CRM Customer Service and Support Software, Worldwide

Published: 11 March 2025


### Summary

The CRM CSS software market will grow from $43.4 billion in 2024 at a constant-currency CAGR of 13.9% through 2028. Growth will be driven by higher user adoption, broader functional adoption and innovation, notably with GenAI-enabled solutions.
