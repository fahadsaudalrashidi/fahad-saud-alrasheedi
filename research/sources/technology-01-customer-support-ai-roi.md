# Generative AI at Work | NBER

- URL: https://www.nber.org/papers/w31161
- Source date: 2023 (NBER Working Paper 31161; verify latest publication status before external use).
- Source tier: Tier 1 — National Bureau of Economic Research working paper; peer-reviewed publication thereafter not verified here.
- Caveat: A staggered rollout at one large business; measured issues resolved per hour rather than causal dollar ROI; results are heterogeneous and may not generalize.
- Research question: Find the experimental methods and measured productivity outcomes of the customer-support generative AI field study, including sample, intervention, and limits.

## Extracted findings

The field study involved the following:

*   **Experimental Methods:** The researchers studied the "staggered introduction of a generative AI-based conversational assistant" [L255].
*   **Sample:** The study used data from 5,179 customer support agents [L255].
*   **Intervention:** Access to a generative AI-based conversational assistant tool [L255].
*   **Productivity Outcomes:** Productivity, measured by issues resolved per hour, increased by an average of 14% [L255]. This included a 34% improvement for novice and low-skilled workers, while there was minimal impact on experienced and highly skilled workers [L255]. Additionally, the study found improved customer sentiment, increased employee retention, and evidence of potential worker learning [L255].
*   **Limits:** The study identified "large heterogeneity in effects across workers" [L255].
