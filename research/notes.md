## Plan

- **Question:** Where can a Saudi/MENA-first AI support and operations platform win, and how should it package and price its first enterprise offer?
- **Scope:** Public evidence on global support/ESM vendors, Saudi and wider MENA adoption, buyer needs, practical SaaS packaging, and product positioning. No unsupported market-size forecast.
- **Out of scope:** Primary customer interviews, private enterprise quotes, and claims about the product's actual traction.

## Findings Log

_[@key] markers reference the source registry and are linked in the in-app module._

- Gartner's worldwide CRM customer-service/support forecast is $43.4B for 2024 with 13.9% constant-currency CAGR through 2028. It is not a Saudi or all-ESM market size. [@gartner-crm-css-forecast]
- Gartner's 2025 customer survey found 60% said agents fail to promote self-service; its customer-facing GenAI figures are exploratory intent, not adoption or realized savings. [@gartner-service-survey]
- Saudi digital-government indices and national AI initiatives provide policy and modernization context, not proof of private-sector support-software demand. [@saudi-dga-indicators] [@sdaia-ai-strategy] [@vision2030-ntp]
- A single-company field study reported 14% more support issues resolved per hour on average, with heterogeneous effects; it does not establish dollar ROI for this product. [@nber-ai-work]
- Public vendor examples show seat-based subscriptions and outcome/usage charges, but published foreign prices are dynamic and not validated Saudi willingness-to-pay. [@intercom-pricing] [@freshworks-pricing] [@zendesk-pricing]
- GCC integration complexity and wider-MENA implementation cost are procurement risks to scope, not published local fee schedules. [@gsma-gcc] [@imf-gcc]
- The product wedge, personas, SWOT, and package tiers are hypotheses. Recommendations prioritize a measurable pilot, native-language evaluation, human-supervised escalation, and measured unit economics.
- Research date: 2026-10-27. The in-app strategy view returns Arabic and English narratives; linked source titles remain in their original published language.
